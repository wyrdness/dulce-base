## Overview

Dulce Base is the name given to an alleged secret underground facility supposedly located beneath Archuleta Mesa near the town of Dulce, in northwestern New Mexico. According to conspiracy proponents, the facility is a joint US-government and extraterrestrial laboratory housing horrific human and animal experiments on multiple subterranean levels. The narrative has its origins in the activities of Albuquerque businessman Paul Bennewitz (1927–2003), who in July 1979 began self-funded UFO investigations and came to believe he was intercepting alien communications near Kirtland Air Force Base. In May 1980 hypnosis sessions with the alleged abductee Myrna Hansen, conducted by psychologist R. Leo Sprinkle, generated narratives of underground bases with 'body parts floating in vats'. Bennewitz contacted Kirtland AFB on 24 October 1980, and on 2 December 1981 wrote to Senator Pete Domenici describing a 'Dulce War' he placed in late 1979 or early 1980. UFOlogist William Moore publicly admitted on 1 July 1989 that he and US Air Force Office of Special Investigations officer Richard Doty had run a deliberate disinformation campaign against Bennewitz; CIA-archived materials from 1992 corroborated the campaign. Bennewitz was hospitalized for psychiatric care in August 1988. The narrative was further amplified in 1987 by John Lear, in 1988 by sensational coverage in the Weekly World News, and through pseudonymous figures including 'Jason Bishop III' (Thomas Allen LeVesque), who later acknowledged fabricating his accounts. No physical evidence, photographs, or documentary records of the alleged facility have ever surfaced. Political scientist Michael Barkun has noted that real Cold War-era missile and military installations in the region lent superficial credibility to the rumours, but characterizes the alien-firefight claims as far outside even the most aggressive secret-base claims. Dulce locals have at times leaned into the legend for tourism, hosting a Dulce Base UFO Conference in 2016.

## Theories & Explanations

### Paranormal / Cryptozoological

**Joint US-government / extraterrestrial laboratory** _(Paul Bennewitz, John Lear, 'Jason Bishop III' (Thomas Allen LeVesque))_

Conspiracy claim that Dulce Base is a multi-level underground facility where the US government and extraterrestrials jointly conduct experiments on humans and animals.

### Skeptical

**AFOSI disinformation operation** _(William Moore (1989 disclosure), Michael Barkun)_

The Dulce Base narrative is best explained as the product of a deliberate disinformation campaign conducted by the US Air Force Office of Special Investigations (AFOSI), via William Moore and Richard Doty, against Paul Bennewitz from 1980 onwards.

**Acknowledged fabrication** _(Thomas Allen LeVesque (acknowledged after the fact))_

Thomas Allen LeVesque, who wrote pseudonymously as 'Jason Bishop III', later acknowledged that he had fabricated key Dulce Base accounts.

**No physical or documentary evidence** _(Mainstream skeptical literature)_

After more than four decades and intensive UFO-community investigation, no specimen, photograph, satellite signature, or government document of the alleged Dulce Base has surfaced.
